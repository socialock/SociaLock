Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create a post?

Tap "Why don’t write thoughts?" at the top of your Home feed, write up to 300 characters, then tap Post.

How do I follow or block someone?

Open their profile from any post or search result. You'll find Follow and Block options right there.

Why don't I see new posts right away?

Your Home feed stays exactly as you left it — like Facebook — so scrolling back doesn't lose your place. Tap the Home icon again, or pull to refresh, to load new posts.

How do I delete a post or comment?

Tap the ••• menu on your own post, or the delete icon on your comment, and confirm the removal.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and follow the instructions sent to your email.
